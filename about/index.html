ABOUT

Francesco Maccario was born in Alba in 1997. Chronologically: guitarist during his years at art high school, then electroacoustic composer at the conservatories of Bologna and Turin, sound technician for conferences, film projectionist.
Artistically: multimedia experimenter, he compensates for serious structural shortcomings and an unpleasant lack of a true language through layers of pseudo-casual tricks and arbitrary transformations.
